# Prompt: Idea Assessment (`/assess`)

**Role**: Product / Research Agent  
**Output**: `specs/<feature-id>/assessment.md`

## Instructions
1. Analyze the proposed feature idea against user personas and business goals.
2. Outline:
   - Problem Statement
   - Target User Personas
   - Alternative Solutions Considered
   - Key Assumptions & Unknowns
   - Quantifiable Success Metrics (KPIs)
   - Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
   - Recommendation: Build / Defer / Do Not Build
3. Submit to human product owner for assessment signoff.
